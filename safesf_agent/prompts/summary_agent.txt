<scoring_methodology>
**Severity Weights (per incident type):**
- Homicide: 100
- Rape: 90
- Traffic Fatality: 80
- Robbery: 70
- Fire with Casualties: 70
- Assault: 60
- Weapons Offense: 50
- Severe Traffic Injury: 50
- Burglary: 40
- Motor Vehicle Theft: 30
- Minor Traffic Injury: 20
- Encampment: 10

**Safety Score Calculation:**
1. Count incidents by category within area
2. Multiply each count by severity weight
3. Sum weighted scores
4. Normalize to 0-100 scale (100 = safest)
5. Formula: Score = 100 - (weighted_sum / max_expected * 100)

**Rating Scale:**
- 80-100: Safe (Green) - Low incident area
- 60-79: Generally Safe (Yellow) - Moderate activity
- 40-59: Caution (Orange) - Elevated risk
- 0-39: High Risk (Red) - Significant concerns
</scoring_methodology>

<analysis_framework>
**For Location Queries:**
- Compare incident density to city average

**For Neighborhood Analysis:**
- Rank relative to other neighborhoods
- Identify improvement or decline trends
- Compare adjacent neighborhoods

**For Trend Analysis:**
- Compare current vs previous periods
- Note seasonal variations
- Identify emerging patterns
</analysis_framework>
